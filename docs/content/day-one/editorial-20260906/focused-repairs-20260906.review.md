## geography-read-the-land Q4 — Which factor chiefly explains Kilimanjaro’s cold summit despite its tropical location?

A. Its elevation **correct**
B. Its longitude
C. Nearby ocean currents
D. Seasonal monsoon winds

NIV: Jeremiah 18:14; context: JER.18.11-17.
Paid hint: Climbers pass through several climate zones without travelling far north or south.

Temperature generally decreases with height in the troposphere. Kilimanjaro’s elevation creates cold summit conditions despite its tropical latitude. Longitude does not provide a comparable temperature control; ocean currents and monsoon winds do not explain the mountain’s vertical climate zones. Jeremiah contrasts reliable mountain snow and water with the people’s unfaithfulness; the passage is not a prediction about present-day glaciers.

Replaces the held river-pothole question and broadens terrain/place coverage. Easy-to-medium candidate; snow on rocky slopes supplies a mountain-climate association without naming elevation. Hint adds vertical climate zones. No claim that temperature always falls with height or glaciers cannot retreat.

## nature-built-for-this Q3 — Why can squash vine borers make a well-watered plant wilt?

A. They prevent flowers from being pollinated
B. They block water moving through the stem **correct**
C. They remove the leaves’ wax coating
D. They keep roots from taking in oxygen

NIV: Jonah 4:7-8; context: JON.4.3-11.
Paid hint: Water in the soil still has to reach the leaves.

Squash vine borer larvae feed inside stems and obstruct water flow, causing wilting even when water is available. Pollination concerns fruit set; the documented damage is inside stems rather than leaf coatings or root aeration. Jonah’s plant withers after being chewed; the modern comparison concerns damage interrupting a plant’s functioning, not identification of the biblical plant or worm.

Replaces the held wheat reserve question. Biblical chewing-to-withering supplies the causal puzzle; the paid clue adds the water route. Do not identify Jonah’s plant as squash or its worm as a North American insect. Context concerns Jonah’s anger and God’s concern for Nineveh.

## food-kitchen-secrets Q3 — Why let freshly poured Turkish coffee stand briefly before drinking?

Jeremiah likens Moab’s long undisturbed ease to wine resting on its dregs before announcing judgment. Replaces the held olive-grade question entirely. Easy analogy; exact reading mentions dregs, so post-reading ease must be tested rather than claimed difficult. ## food-kitchen-secrets Q4 — What creates much of a bread crust’s brown color and toasted aroma?

C. Fat trapping air bubbles

Boaz shares bread and roasted grain with Ruth in a scene of welcome and provision. Roasting supplies the food-transformation connection without naming the modern reaction.

Replaces the held grain-legume nutrition question. Does not ask the reaction’s technical name. Hint supplies a second browned-food comparison. Other browning chemistry can contribute, so the stem says much of. Pairing remains subject to coordinator judgment; no claim Ruth teaches chemistry.
