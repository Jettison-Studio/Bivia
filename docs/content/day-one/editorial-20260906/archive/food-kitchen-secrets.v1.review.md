## 3. Which step is excluded when making extra virgin olive oil?

A. Washing the olives  
B. Filtering the oil  
C. Separating oil in a centrifuge  
D. Refining the oil **(correct)**  

NIV reading: **Micah 6:15**. Context checked: `MIC.6.9-16`.

Original paid hint: Think of keeping the oil as it comes from the fruit.

Extra virgin oil is a category of virgin oil obtained by physical methods, without refining. Washing, centrifugation and filtration are permitted. Micah describes pressing olives in a judgment against injustice: labor will not yield enjoyment of its products. The verse does not assign a modern oil grade.

**coordinator_revision_required:** Pressing does not distinguish virgin from refined oil. The hint can wrongly steer players away from permitted filtering and centrifugation. Negative stem needs playtest. Mechanical pressing is the specific bridge, but the reading cannot establish the full modern standard. Do not claim zero heat or that all mechanically obtained oil is extra virgin.

Sources: [source 1](https://www.internationaloliveoil.org/olive-world/olive-oil/).
