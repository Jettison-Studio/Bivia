## Q1. What gives mountain goats extra grip on bare rock?

A. Hard, smooth soles
B. Soft pads inside hard hoof rims **correct**
C. Deep grooves in the hoof walls
D. Long toes that dig into rock

NIV: Psalm 104:18; context None
Paid hint: Picture the sole of a climbing shoe.

Mountain goats have pliable split hooves with soft inner pads and a hard outer lining. This combination helps grip rock. Hard smooth soles, deep hoof-wall grooves and toes digging into solid rock are not the documented traction mechanism. Psalm 104 praises habitats provided for animals, without identifying a North American species or describing hoof anatomy.

## Q3. Why can squash vine borers make a well-watered plant wilt?

A. They prevent flowers from being pollinated
B. They block water moving through the stem **correct**
C. They remove the leaves’ wax coating
D. They keep roots from taking in oxygen

NIV: Jonah 4:7-8; context JON.4.3-11
Paid hint: Water in the soil still has to reach the leaves.

Squash vine borer larvae feed inside stems and obstruct water flow, causing wilting even when water is available. Pollination concerns fruit set; the documented damage is inside stems rather than leaf coatings or root aeration. Jonah’s plant withers after being chewed; the modern comparison concerns damage interrupting a plant’s functioning, not identification of the biblical plant or worm.

## Q4. Which cue helps many migrating birds sense autumn, even while the weather stays warm?

A. A full moon

Changes in day length help many birds time seasonal migration. Weather and food also influence actual departures. Jeremiah contrasts birds observing their seasons with people failing to heed God; he does not describe the physiological mechanism.

## Q5. What do aspen stems grown from one tree’s roots usually share?

A. The same age
B. The same height
C. The same lifespan
D. The same genetic makeup **correct**

NIV: Isaiah 11:1-3; context None
Paid hint: This growth skips pollen and seeds.

Root sprouts are vegetative offspring of one aspen and ordinarily share its genetic makeup. Stems can arise at different times and experience different growth or survival, so age, height and lifespan need not match. Somatic mutations do not justify a claim that every cell is forever identical. Isaiah uses new growth as a Davidic ruler image, not aspen biology.
