## Q2. Which note in a finely printed book may identify its printer, paper and typeface?

B. An epigraph
C. A glossary
D. A colophon **correct**

NIV: Romans 16:22; context ROM.16.21-27
Paid hint: Think of the closing credits of a film, applied to a printed book.

A colophon can record production details and those responsible for making a book. The greeting is not called a modern colophon in the question or explanation. An index locates topics, an epigraph supplies an opening quotation, and a glossary explains terms.
